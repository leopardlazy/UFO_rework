06 / WHAT WE DO

End-to-End Production Services

We handle everything from camera setups and live arena floors to celebrity coordination and feature films.

Floor & Broadcast Management

Operating under the high-pressure demands of televised broadcasting. Multi-camera switching protocols, live audience choreography, and real-time contingency response.

Celebrity & Talent Management

End-to-end artist coordination. Contract riders, green-room protocols, direct security escorts, and customized stage choreography across Bollywood and sports celebrities.

International Film Co-Production

We facilitate seamless international shoots from pre-production to on-ground execution, covering key locations across Europe (Paris, Cannes, Barcelona, Madrid), Vietnam, the Maldives, Dubai, and the broader Gulf region. Our end-to-end services include location research, permits, budgeting, scheduling, travel logistics, crew and equipment sourcing, and live shoot management—delivering tailored production plans aligned with your destination, schedule, creative brief, and budget.

Brand & Influencer Activations

Connecting luxury hospitality properties, fashion labels, and consumer brands with targeted talent for experiential marketing and high-engagement social media campaigns.

Corporate Summits & Galas

Staging high-stakes annual general meetings, corporate award ceremonies, milestone galas, and immersive tech product reveals with cutting-edge AV design.

Bespoke Destination Weddings

Curating luxury weddings where grand hospitality meets cinematic staging. Flawless schedule execution, world-class artist bookings, and memory-making elegance.
